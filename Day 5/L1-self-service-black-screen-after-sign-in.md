# Black Screen After You Sign In - Help You Can Do Yourself

Version header: v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft

If your screen stays black after you sign in, do not worry. This is often short-term, and your files and saved work in your company account are safe.

## Steps you can do now

1. Wait 30 to 60 seconds.
Sometimes your work screen appears after a short wait.

2. Sign out, then sign in again.
Open the program menu, sign out fully, wait 20 seconds, then sign in again.

3. Close and open the work access program again.
After it opens, sign in and try again.

4. Restart your computer.
This can fix a short-term internet or sign-in problem.

5. Check your internet connection.
Make sure your internet is working well (Wi-Fi or cable). If you can, move to a better connection and try again.

6. Try one last sign-in.
If the screen is still black, stop trying and contact the service desk.

## What to include when you contact the service desk

Please include these details in your support request:

- Your full name
- Your work email address
- Best phone number to reach you
- The time the issue started (and your local time area)
- What you see on screen (example: "black screen after sign-in")
- If it happens every time or only sometimes
- Which steps from this article you already tried
- A screen photo, if you can take one
- Your location (office or home) and how you are connected (Wi-Fi, cable, or phone hotspot)

Your data is safe. The support team will guide you and help you get back to work.

Contact the service desk if the screen is still black after these steps, or if the problem comes back after a normal sign-in.
