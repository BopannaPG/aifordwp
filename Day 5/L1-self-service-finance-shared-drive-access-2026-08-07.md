# Finance Shared Folder Access Help
Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

Your data is safe. If your Finance shared folder is missing or not opening, use the steps below.

## What you can do now
1. Save your work in any open programs.
2. Sign out of Windows fully (do not just lock the screen).
3. Wait 60 seconds.
4. Sign in again.
5. Open the folder window (File Explorer).
6. Check if the S: Finance folder shortcut is visible.
7. Open the S: Finance folder and confirm your folders load.
8. If it works, continue working as normal.
9. If it still does not work, wait 2 minutes and repeat Steps 2 to 7 one more time.

## If the issue is still there
Please contact the Service Desk and share the details below so support can fix it faster.

## Details to include in your ticket
- Your full name
- Your work email
- Your computer name (for example DESKTOP-FB041)
- Office location or whether you are working from home
- The exact time the issue happened
- What you saw (for example: S: Finance folder missing, or folder opens but contents do not load)
- Whether this happened after sign-in or during the day
- A screen photo of the message, if shown
- Whether trying sign out and sign in again helped

## Important note
This issue does not mean your files are lost. Your files remain stored safely.

Contact the Service Desk if the S: Finance folder is still missing or not opening after you sign out, wait, and sign in again.
