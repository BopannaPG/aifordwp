## What's Happening?

After recent computer updates, a small number of staff in Floor 6 Legal may see client cases in their search results that they shouldn't be able to see. If you notice a case appearing in your search that you've never worked on—that's what we're fixing.

**Important:** Your data is completely safe. Nothing has been deleted or shared. This is a system error from the update that's showing you access to cases you shouldn't see.

---

## How to Check If This Affects You

1. **Open the search box** in Microsoft 365 (the search feature at the top of your screen)
2. **Search for a case name** that you know you should NOT be working on
3. **Look at the results:**
   - ✓ **Good:** The case doesn't appear in results (you don't have access—this is normal)
   - ✗ **Problem:** The case appears in results (this shouldn't happen)

If you see a case you've never worked on, you may have found the issue.

---

## What You Can Do Right Now

### Step 1: Restart Your Computer
- **Save your work first**
- Sign out of everything
- Shut down your computer completely
- Wait 30 seconds
- Turn it back on
- Sign in using your regular username and password

This often fixes the problem automatically.

### Step 2: Clear Stored Search Data (if the case still appears)
1. Open the search box in Microsoft 365
2. Look for **Settings** (usually in the top-right corner)
3. Select **Clear Search History** or **Clear Stored Data**
4. Close and reopen the search tool
5. Try searching for that case again—it should no longer appear

### Step 3: Wait
- It can take up to 4 hours for the system to update your access
- The system updates automatically throughout the day
- Try searching again in 30 minutes

---

## When to Contact the Service Desk

**Call us if:**
- You see a client case in your search that you've never worked on, AND
- You've restarted your computer and cleared your stored data, AND
- You've waited at least 30 minutes and the case STILL appears
