# Self-Service Guide: Slow or Blocked Sign-In

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 14/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

---

## What's Happening?

On Monday morning, some people on Floor 6 had trouble signing in to their computers or found it was taking a very long time. We discovered this was caused by a new program that was added to computers on Friday afternoon. We've removed that program, and your computer should work normally now.

**Your data is completely safe** — nothing has been deleted or lost.

---

## What You Can Do Right Now

### Step 1: Turn Off and Back On Your Computer (2 minutes)

1. Click the Windows logo button in the bottom-left corner
2. Click the Power icon (it looks like a circle with a line)
3. Click **Restart**
4. Your computer will automatically turn off and back on (this takes 3-5 minutes)

### Step 2: Wait for Your Computer to Receive New Settings (up to 1 hour)

After your computer restarts, it will automatically receive and apply new instructions from the office. This normally takes 15-60 minutes.

**During this time:**
- Your computer is working normally — you can use email, Internet, and all your programs
- You might see a pop-up message on the bottom-right corner asking you to restart again (this is normal; you can restart when you're ready)

### Step 3: Try Signing In Again

Once your computer has turned back on:
1. Turn on your computer (if it's off)
2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Delete (hold Ctrl and Alt, then press Delete)
3. Enter your username and password

**Expected result:** You should sign in within 10-30 seconds (not several minutes).

---

## If You're Still Having Problems

### Before You Call

Try these one more time:
1. **Turn off and back on your computer again**
2. **Wait at least 1 hour after restarting** before trying to sign in
3. **Try later** — if you restart in the morning but have problems at noon, try again in the afternoon

### When to Contact the Service Desk

Call extension 4357 (on your work phone) or email servicedesk@finbridge.local if:
- You've waited more than 1 hour and sign-in is still very slow
- You cannot sign in at all (a message appears saying something went wrong)
- You've restarted twice and the problem continues

### What to Tell the Service Desk

When you contact them, please tell them:
- **Your name and email address**
- **Your computer's name** (to find this: click the Windows logo > Settings > System > About > look for "Device name")
- **When the problem started** (Monday morning)
- **What happens when you try to sign in** (very slow / completely blocked / error message)
- **How many times you've restarted your computer**
- **Any exact message text you see** (copy and paste it if you can)
