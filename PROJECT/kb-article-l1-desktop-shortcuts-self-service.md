# Desktop Icons Missing - Quick Help You Can Do Yourself

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 14/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your desktop icons are not showing, you are not alone. This can happen after a normal computer update. Your files and work documents are safe.

## What you can do now

1. Save your work.
2. Go to your desktop and press the Refresh key once (F5 on the top row of your keyboard).
3. Wait 2 minutes and check if the icons return.
4. If they are still missing, restart your computer:
   - Select Start
   - Select Power
   - Select Restart
5. After you log in, wait 5 minutes and check the desktop again.
6. If still missing, log out and log back in one time.

## Important

- Do not delete folders or create new desktop icons yet.
- In most cases, the icons are hidden for a short time. Your files are not deleted.
- Your original files are expected to remain safe.

## Details to include when you ask for help

Please share these details when contacting the IT Help Desk:

- Your full name
- Your work email
- Your device name (if known)
- Your office floor and team name (example: Floor 6 Legal)
- Approximate time the icons disappeared
- Whether it happened after restart or update
- Whether coworkers have the same issue
- One icon name that is missing
- What you already tried (Refresh key F5, restart, log out and log back in)

Contact the Service Desk if icons are still missing after one restart and one log out and log back in.
