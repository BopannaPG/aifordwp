## Top 3 Themes to Act On Today (Severity-Weighted Ranking)

### 1) AVD sign-in and account lockouts (Count: 7)
Why it ranks here:
- This is the highest-volume Blocker theme and directly prevents users from starting work at all.
- It combines two hard-stop conditions (cannot sign in and lockout), creating immediate business interruption.

One sentence for manager:
- "AVD sign-in/lockout is our top priority today because it has the largest blocker footprint and is stopping multiple users from working entirely."

### 2) Missing files or OneDrive file availability concerns (Count: 5)
Why it ranks here:
- This is a Blocker because users report missing business files needed for meetings and deadlines.
- Although lower count than AVD lockouts, potential data availability risk and deadline impact make it urgent.

One sentence for manager:
- "OneDrive/missing-file incidents are our second priority because users cannot access critical working documents, creating immediate delivery and client risk."

### 3) Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance) (Count: 3)
Why it ranks here:
- This remains a Blocker despite lower volume, because affected users cannot access core finance shares and month-end artifacts.
- A small number of blockers outrank higher-volume minor/friction issues due to direct inability to perform role-critical tasks.

One sentence for manager:
- "Shared-drive access failures rank third because they block finance workflows outright, even with fewer reports than lower-severity issues."

## Why Higher-Count Non-Blockers Were Not in Top 3
- Floor 3 printer mapping failures (6) are high-friction and important, but users still have workaround behavior (e.g., printing on another floor), so it ranks below blocker themes.
- Cosmetic/UI changes (7) have high count but low business impact and therefore do not outrank blocker categories.
