Subject: Service Notice - AVD Sign-in and Account Lockouts

Team,

We are aware of an issue this morning affecting AVD sign-in for a growing number of users. This is a known issue and our support teams are actively working on it now.

As an interim workaround, please try:
1. Close the AVD app/browser window fully, reopen it, and sign in again slowly. Double-check your username and password before you submit.
2. If it still fails, wait 10 minutes, then try once more to avoid repeated lock attempts.

Please avoid repeated rapid sign-in attempts, as this can cause additional lockouts.

We will post the next update within 60 minutes, even if we are still investigating.

Thank you for your patience. We understand this is disruptive, and we are treating it as a priority.

If you still cannot sign in after the steps above, contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact error message, time of last failed attempt, and whether you are blocked for an urgent meeting or deadline.
