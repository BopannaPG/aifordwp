## AVD sign-in and account lockouts
Please try these steps:
1. Close the AVD app/browser window fully, reopen it, and sign in again slowly. Double-check your username and password before you submit.
2. If it still fails, wait 10 minutes, then try once more to avoid repeated lock attempts.

If you still cannot sign in, contact the Service Desk right away. Share: exact error message on screen, time of your last failed attempt, your username, and whether this is blocking an urgent meeting or deadline.

## Missing files or OneDrive file availability concerns
Please try these steps:
1. Open OneDrive and check if it says signed in. If not, sign in with your work account and let it sync for a few minutes.
2. Use Windows search to look for the exact file name in case it moved folders during migration.

If files are still missing, contact the Service Desk immediately. Share: missing file/folder names, where they were stored before, when you last saw them, and any deadline (for example, client meeting or report due time).

## Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance)
Please try these steps:
1. Disconnect and reconnect to your office network/VPN, then open File Explorer and try the S: drive again.
2. Sign out of Windows, sign back in, and retry access once.

If access is still denied, contact the Service Desk. Share: the full message you see, your username, your team/department, the drive name (S:/Finance), and what work is currently blocked (for example, month-end report).
