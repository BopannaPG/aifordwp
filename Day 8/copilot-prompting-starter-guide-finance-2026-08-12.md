# Copilot Prompting Starter Guide (FinBridge)

*For non-technical office staff | Day 8 | 2026-08-12*

This guide is designed to help you get useful results from Microsoft 365 Copilot quickly, without needing technical language.

## Start Here: The 5-Part Prompt Formula

Use this simple pattern every time:

**Task + Context + Source + Format + Checks**

1. **Task**: What you want Copilot to do
2. **Context**: Why you need it / who it is for
3. **Source**: Which emails/files/data to use
4. **Format**: How the output should look
5. **Checks**: Any limits, tone, or things to avoid

### Copy-and-reuse template

"Help me **[task]** for **[audience/purpose]**. Use **[source]**. Format as **[output format]**. Keep it **[tone/length/rules]**."

## Outlook Prompt Examples (Real Use)

### 1) Inbox catch-up after being away

"Summarise the emails I received from Monday to Wednesday from the Finance Leadership Team. Group by topic, highlight decisions needed from me, and list urgent actions with due dates in a table."

### 2) Draft a clear reply to a client request

"Draft a reply to the latest email from Greenfield Advisory about the Q4 invoice query. Confirm we received their message, explain we are reviewing the figures, and propose a call this week. Keep the tone professional and under 140 words."

### 3) Prepare for a meeting from email history

"Look at my emails with Orion Capital from the last 30 days and give me a simple meeting prep note: what is still outstanding, what we said we would do, upcoming deadlines, and 5 useful questions for today\'s call."

---

## Word Prompt Examples (Real Use)

### 1) Turn rough notes into a polished document

"Turn these meeting notes into a one-page project update for senior managers. Use headings: Progress, Risks, Decisions, Next Steps. Keep the language plain and business-friendly."

### 2) Rewrite for clarity and tone

"Rewrite this draft policy update so it is easier for non-technical staff to understand. Keep the meaning the same, shorten long sentences, and add a short \"What this means for you\" section at the end."

### 3) Create a first draft from bullet points

"Using the bullet points in this document, draft a client-facing status update email we can send on Friday. Keep it confident but not over-promising, and include a short action list at the end."

---

## Excel Prompt Examples (Real Use)

### 1) Find trends in monthly spend

"Analyse this sheet for monthly travel spend by department. Identify the top 3 departments with the biggest increase versus last quarter, and explain possible reasons in plain language."

### 2) Build a quick summary for managers

"Summarise this workbook with: total overdue invoices, overdue amounts split into 0-30 days, 31-60 days, and 61+ days, plus the top 10 overdue clients by amount. Show it in a clear table I can share with my manager."

### 3) Help with data quality checks

"Check this worksheet for common mistakes: missing account IDs, duplicate invoice numbers, and dates outside this financial year. Then give me a simple step-by-step plan to clean it up."

## Make Prompts Better in 10 Seconds

If your first result is too generic, add one more line:

- "Use only the files/emails I have open."
- "Ask me up to 3 follow-up questions before writing the draft."
- "Give me 2 versions: short and detailed."

Small detail in, better result out.

## Data Safety Reminder (Day 1 Habit)

From **Day 1** of this programme: protect client information by default.

- Use **approved FinBridge Microsoft 365 Copilot** for work content.
- Do **not** paste confidential client data, account numbers, or sensitive case details into **external AI tools** (public chatbots, unapproved websites, personal AI apps).
- If you are not sure whether a tool is approved, pause and check with IT first.

This is not about avoiding Copilot. It is about using the right tool in the right place so you can work safely and confidently.

## Quick Daily Use Checklist

Before you hit Enter on a prompt, check:

1. Did I clearly say the task?
2. Did I name the source (emails, file, sheet)?
3. Did I ask for the format I need?
4. Did I remove any unnecessary sensitive detail?

If yes, you are ready.
